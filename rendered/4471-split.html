#4471 split · what the merged dates ticket holds

owner support desk open reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

#4471 says one issue and one fix. Its reports say otherwise. Merging on the subject line (decision 0001) put every “wrong day” report in one ticket, whatever broke the date. Read by tenant, by booking form and by what the reporter describes, #4471 holds more than one problem, and each needs its own fix.

The queued parser fix does not close #4471. It leaves the booking-v3 form hint and the one-day shifts open.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant gets day and month swapped through the app, by email, and through booking-v2’s form. This is the fix #4471 already queued.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so the tenant’s people type month first, and the form reads the tenant’s way, day first. Fixing the parser does not touch this. The hint has to match how the form reads.
  • shift: the reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk notes). The date lands one day early or late, and the same meeting shows on different days for people in different places. A month-first parser cannot do that: it swaps day and month, it does not move a date by one. It looks like a time zone problem, not a format problem.

How each report was placed

  1. One id, one report: the export repeats some rows, and each id counts once.
  2. The desk notes come first: a report the desk read as a one-day move is shift.
  3. Otherwise the tenant decides (causes.ts): a day-first tenant on booking-v3’s form is hint, every other day-first report is parser.
  4. A report with no tenant on file, or from a month-first tenant, stays unplaced.
